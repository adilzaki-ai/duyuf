# Partner logos on the Duyuf cover

The cover has two spots: **top left** for the Saudi Vision 2030 logo and
**top centre** for the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah logo.

These are official government marks. Put a file here **only with written
permission** from the owner (Vision 2030's brand rules; the Ministry), in the
form they supply:

- `vision2030.svg` (or `.png`, `.jpg`) — top left (Adil's chosen `.jpg`, the white logo on its own photo background, is in place in a rounded frame)
- `mohu.svg` (or `.png`) — top centre

The cover finds them by name; nothing else changes. Without them it shows a
line of text in each spot, never an imitation of the logo.
